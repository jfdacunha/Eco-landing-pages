Warning Signs

Why a Dirty Chimney Is a Fire Risk

Creosote and soot build up every time you light a fire. Left untreated, it restricts airflow, causes smoke to back up into your home and significantly increases the risk of a chimney fire.

  • ! Creosote buildup is the leading cause of residential chimney fires
  • ! Blocked flues cause smoke and carbon monoxide to back up indoors
  • ! Soot stains and odours spread through your living space
  • ! Reduced airflow makes your fireplace inefficient and smoky
  • ! Undetected cracks or blockages can go unnoticed without inspection
