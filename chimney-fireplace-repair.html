If the mortar joints are cracked or crumbling but the bricks are intact, repointing is usually sufficient. If the bricks themselves are spalling, cracked or the stack is leaning, a partial or full rebuild is needed. We inspect and advise honestly and will never recommend more than what is needed.

Yes. Balanced flue gas fireplaces can be installed without an existing chimney as they vent directly through an external wall. For traditional flue systems, we can install a new flue liner and housing. We will advise the best option for your home and budget.

Chimney leaks usually come from failed flashing, a cracked or missing chimney cap, deteriorated crown mortar, or cracked brickwork absorbing water. We inspect the whole system and identify the exact source before quoting any repairs.

A standard gas fireplace installation typically takes one to two days. Wood fireplace installations with new flue systems can take two to three days depending on complexity. We give you a clear timeline before starting so you can plan accordingly.

Gas fireplace installations require a licensed gas fitter and the work must be certified. Some council areas may require a Development Application for solid fuel heaters. We handle the compliance requirements and advise you on any council requirements upfront.

Chimney repointing costs vary depending on the size of the stack and the extent of deterioration. Most single stack residential jobs range from $800 to $2,500. We provide a free no obligation quote after inspecting so you know exactly what you are getting and what it costs.
